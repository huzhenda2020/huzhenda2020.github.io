About

Research profile

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE) in 2023, through a joint program with Singapore Management University (SMU), under the supervision of Prof. Yinglin Wang, and Prof. Zhaoxia Wang during the joint-training period at Singapore Management University.

My research interests cover large language models (LLMs), natural language processing (NLP), deep learning, affective computing, and financial time series forecasting. My recent projects focus on LLMs, NLP, agents and its financial applications, intelligent dialogue and intent recognition, and financial time series forecasting. I have published 10+ papers in SCI-indexed journals and CCF international conferences, including Expert Systems with Applications, Neural Computing and Applications, and IJCNN.
